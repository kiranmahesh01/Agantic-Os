# Agency OS

Self-hosted SaaS stack for agency/freelance builders who want to own their infrastructure.

## What's inside

- **[SAAS_STACK.md](docs/SAAS_STACK.md)** — Curated list of 90+ open-source alternatives to expensive SaaS subscriptions

## Why self-host?

Trade monthly SaaS fees for setup time and VPS costs. Best for:
- Agencies building multiple client projects
- Freelancers tired of subscription creep
- Teams that want full control over their data

## Categories covered

- Frontend & UI (Next.js, React, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion)
- Backend & Database (Convex, MinIO, Supabase)
- Auth (Better Auth, NextAuth, Lucia, Keycloak)
- Analytics & Monitoring (PostHog, Sentry, Uptime Kuma, Plausible)
- Email & Communication (Listmonk, Chatwoot, Formbricks)
- Social Publishing (Postiz, Mixpost)
- Automation (n8n, Plane, Trigger.dev)
- Deployment (Coolify, Dokku, Kamal)
- AI & Agents (MCP SDK, Claude Agent SDK, Dify, Langfuse)
- Media & Rendering (FFmpeg, Remotion, ComfyUI, Fish Speech)
- Testing (Playwright, Vitest)
- Documentation (Docusaurus, Nextra)
