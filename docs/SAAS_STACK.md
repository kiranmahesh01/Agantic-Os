## Cost Optimization Notes

**Self-hosting vs Managed SaaS** — rough monthly estimates (as of 2026-09, unverified):

- **Vercel Pro**: ~$20/mo → Coolify on $6 VPS
- **Clerk**: ~$25/mo → Better Auth (self-hosted)
- **ElevenLabs**: ~$22/mo → Fish Speech (self-hosted)
- **Buffer**: ~$15/mo → Postiz (self-hosted)
- **Intercom**: ~$74/mo → Chatwoot (self-hosted)
- **Linear**: ~$10/seat/mo → Plane (self-hosted)
- **Better Stack**: ~$18/mo → Uptime Kuma (self-hosted)
- **Mailchimp**: ~$20/mo → Listmonk (self-hosted)

**Note:** Prices are approximate snapshots, not verified live rates. Self-hosting trades monthly fees for setup time, maintenance burden, and VPS costs.

## Recommended Stacks

### Fastest to MVP
**Open SaaS** (Wasp) — Auth + Billing + AI out of the box

### Most Battle-Tested
**Supabase** + **Next.js** + **Stripe**

### Best Architecture Reference
**Cal.com** — multi-tenancy, billing, extensibility at scale
